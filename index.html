Who we are

Where South America's next football powerhouse is built.

Best Eleven Sports Group is an operating multi-club platform: controlled clubs in South America's three deepest talent markets, plus a gateway into the US market.

The world's leading multi-club groups built the destination: flagship clubs and bridge structures in Europe. What none of them built is the source. Best Eleven is that piece: the South American talent supply base, operating today, with proven exits and two decades of institutional relationships.

Operators, not spectators: proprietary scouting, professional sporting direction and development infrastructure in every market where we compete.

The vision

Europe built the destination. We build the source.

The world's leading multi-club groups face the same structural gap: access to South American talent at the source. Early visibility before the market prices players, local operating structures, and talent shaped to professional European standards.

We aim to raise every club to the highest possible category and level of competition, and to export the best talent to the rest of the world.
