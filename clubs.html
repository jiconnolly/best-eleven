100% group-owned. Rebuilt from the ground up.

Acquired in November 2025, the club is 100% owned by BESG through a Sociedad Anónima Deportiva, fully financially restructured and operationally independent. Uruguay's 3rd division has gained international prestige: Suárez, Messi, Forlán and Muslera have all invested here.

The territorial focus: western Montevideo, an underdeveloped talent zone where the major clubs do not operate. First-mover advantage in youth identification and recruitment.

The US gateway.

Miami United FC competes in the US 1st semi-professional division and is the platform's strategic anchor in the North American market: a showcase window for South American talent entering US football, in one of the highest concentrations of football capital in the Americas, alongside Inter Miami CF.

The strategic vision: becoming a development partner and MLS Next Pro affiliate, and over the long term positioning as a candidate for a second MLS franchise in Miami. A BESG partner is part of the club's ownership, and the club is fully available to the project.
